# Rabochiy process s Cline

## Kak eto rabotaet
1. V nachale sessii Cline zapuskaet: `node scripts/suggest-next-task.js`
2. Cline predlagaet zadachi v chate
3. Vy vybiraete zadachu (numer)
4. Cline vypolnyet ee
5. Cline zaxuskaid: `node scripts/mark-task-done.js <file> <stroka>`
6. Zadacha otmecaetsy v Obsidian

## Komandy dly vas
- `1`, `2`, `3` ‒ vybraty
- `all` — pokazaty vse
- `skip` — propustit
- `status` — statistika
